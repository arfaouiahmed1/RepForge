Place glTF 2.0 .glb files here — one per exercise (e.g. bench_press.glb, squat.glb, shoulder_press.glb)
Source: Mixamo, Sketchfab, or Blender export. Keep <3MB each, embed no textures >1K.
Referenced in ExerciseSeed glbAsset = "models/<file>.glb" and MuscleModelViewer.
Download example: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Models
On missing file, fallback shows muscle chips (no crash).

Premium models: downloaded via model-manifest.json (like LiteRT) with SHA256, cached to filesDir/models/
